# The Battle We Forgot to Fight - Del Turco

## Summary

Roberto Rosselli Del Turco reflects on why digital editions have not yet fully replaced print, identifying obstacles and proposing strategies.

The main arguments are:

- Lack of Adoption: Despite evident benefits, SDEs are often underused or poorly cited. There is a misconception confusing them with simple digitization projects (like «Google Books»).

- Production Issues: There is a lack of standard, easy-to-use tools («out-of-the-box tools»). Creating an SDE requires technical skills that many philologists lack, creating a barrier to entry.

- Proposed Solutions: He suggests developing accessible tools (citing EVT - Edition Visualization Technology), ensuring long-term sustainability through institutions, and promoting rigorous academic evaluation of digital outputs.

In summary, Rosselli Del Turco urges the community to fight for the scientific recognition and usability of digital editions.
