# The Concept of the Work in the Digital Age - Robinson

## Summary

Peter Robinson argues against the recent trend of focusing solely on documents, advocating for a return to the concept of the «Work».

The key points are:

- Work as Communicative Act: Robinson defines the work not as an abstract ideal but as the sum of «communicative acts» that link various documents and texts.

- Critique of Document-Centrism: He challenges the views of Gabler and Pierazzo that privilege the «text-as-document». He argues that an edition must explain the relationships between documents, not just present them.

- Reconstruction: The digital editor must use computational tools to hypothesize and reconstruct genealogical relationships (including lost witnesses), treating the work as a dynamic entity manifest through documents.

In summary, Robinson sees the digital medium as a way to explore the history of the work through the connections between its witnesses.
