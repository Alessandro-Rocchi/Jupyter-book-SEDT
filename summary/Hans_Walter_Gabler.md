# Editing Text – Editing Work

## Summary

Hans Walter Gabler distinguishes between editing a text and editing a work, emphasizing the processual nature of textuality.

His fundamental concepts are:

- Text vs. Work: «Editing a text» is not the same as «editing a work». The text is a function of the material document, while the work is the complex ensemble of all its texts and variants over time.

- Editorial Process: The edition should not aim for a final authoritative text but should show the text as a «work-in-progress», encompassing genetic and post-publication changes.

- Apparatus as Discourse: Gabler proposes transforming the critical apparatus from a list of variants into a continuous discourse, dynamically relating the various states of the text.

In conclusion, Gabler views the digital edition as a dynamic research site for analyzing the historical spectrum of the work's representations.
