# Barely Beyond the Book?

## Summary

Joris van Zundert offers a critique of the current state of digital editing, arguing that many projects merely imitate the printed book rather than innovating.

The salient points are:

- Paradigmatic Regression: Despite the potential of digital technology, many editions remain «metaphors of the book», replicating the limitations of print instead of utilizing true hypertextuality.+

- Methodological Pidgin: A communication gap exists between textual scholars and computer scientists. This results in a «methodological pidgin» where complex technologies are often reduced to mimicking traditional practices.

- Text as Process: Van Zundert calls for a shift from viewing «text-as-document» to viewing text as a network of data and processes. He urges scholars to embrace a more computational approach to represent the fluidity of text.

In summary, the author laments that technology is often used to "recreate what is already known" rather than to explore new forms of knowledge representation.
