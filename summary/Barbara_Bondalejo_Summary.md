# The Texts We See and the Works We Imagine - Bondalejo

## Summary

Bárbara Bordalejo defines the ontological distinctions between document, text, and work in the digital age.

Her key points are:

- Definitions: The «text of the document» is physically inscribed; «variant states» are the different interpretations; the «work» is a mental concept used to group these manifestations (a «minimal denominator»).

- Critique of the Digital: Bordalejo argues that digital tools have not changed the fundamental goals of textual criticism. She challenges the idea that digital editions are ontologically different from print simply because they focus on documents.

- Copyright: The true revolution of the digital lies in licensing (like Creative Commons), which allows for the reuse and modification of data—something impossible with traditional copyright.

In conclusion, she warns against confusing technical innovation with theoretical change: editing remains a critical act of imagination.
