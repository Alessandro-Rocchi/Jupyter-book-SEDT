# Exogenetic Digital Editing and Enactive Cognition

## Summary

Dirk Van Hulle explores the intersection of genetic criticism and cognitive science, proposing the digital edition as a tool to map the creative mind.

The fundamental concepts are:
    • Extended Mind: Based on the «Extended Mind» hypothesis, Van Hulle suggests that the creative process extends beyond the brain to include the author's environment, notes, and library («environmental factors»).
    • Exogenesis: A genetic edition should include not only drafts (endogenesis) but also «exogenesis»—external sources like the author's personal library and marginalia—to reconstruct the cognitive process.
    • Enactive Cognition: Using the Beckett Digital Manuscript Project as a case study, he shows how linking manuscripts to external sources allows us to visualize the mind in action («enactive cognition»).

In short, the digital edition serves as a model to map the interaction between the author and their cultural environment.
