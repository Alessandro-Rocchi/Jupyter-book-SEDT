# A Protocol for Scholarly Digital Editions? The Italian Point of View

## Summary

Marina Buzzoni discusses the need for a protocol for Scholarly Digital Editions (SDE), integrating the specificities of the Italian philological tradition with digital methodology.

Her main arguments include:
    • Core Requirements: An SDE must satisfy five criteria: the ability to handle large quantities of data, «relationability», interoperability, multimediality, and user interaction.
    • Edition-in-Time: Drawing on Gianfranco Contini, Buzzoni views the edition as a dynamic process («edizione-nel-tempo»). The critical apparatus is not merely a list of variants but the site where the text’s historicity is revealed.
    • Digital Apparatus: She critiques the linear apparatus of print and proposes a hypertextual apparatus that allows variants to be studied «in vivo» (in context) rather than «in vitro» (isolated), overcoming the spatial limitations of the page.

In conclusion, Buzzoni advocates for a protocol that ensures scientific «accountability» while fully exploiting the multimedial potential of the digital medium.
